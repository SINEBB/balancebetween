About

I'm Signe. I study how AI and digital tools can support women through pregnancy and early motherhood, and when they shouldn't.

I'm a researcher with a background in biophysics from the Niels Bohr Institute, experience from digital health at Liva Healthcare, and I'm now a PhD fellow at Høgskulen på Vestlandet in Bergen, part of the EU-funded Bump2Baby&Me+ project. My work combines machine learning with qualitative, woman-centred research, with a particular eye on ethics and transparency.

Balance Between is where I write about what sits in between: data and care, technology and the people it's built for, research and the rest of life. Expect notes from the PhD, thoughts on responsible FemTech, and the occasional detour into yoga, travel and whatever else keeps me steady.
